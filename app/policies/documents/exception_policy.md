# ElectroVault Global - Support Exceptions & Escalation Policy
Document ID: exception_policy_v1
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## Section 1: Conditions Requiring Immediate Human Escalation
An automated resolution workflow must halt and escalate the ticket directly to a human specialist in any of the following scenarios:
1. Threat of legal action, regulatory complaints (FTC, BBB), or credit card chargebacks.
2. Uncooperative customer disputes, allegations of fraud, or identity discrepancies.
3. Total claim or order value exceeding $1,000 (100,000 minor units).
4. No clear policy applies to the customer's unique request.
5. Inconclusive or conflicting evidence (e.g., carrier claims delivered with signature, customer claims stolen).

## Section 2: Escalation Process and Ticket Status
1. When escalation is triggered, the ticket status must be transitioned to ESCALATED with an appropriate priority (HIGH or URGENT).
2. The agent must record a comprehensive internal note detailing what facts were verified, what was missing, and the exact reason for escalation.
3. The customer must be informed transparently that a senior specialist will review their case within 1 business day.
