# ElectroVault Global - Shipping & Tracking Inquiries Policy
Document ID: shipping_delay_policy_v1
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## Section 1: Standard Transit Times and Tracking Updates
1. Standard shipping transit times are 3 to 5 business days from carrier pickup.
2. Orders marked as SHIPPED will have an active tracking number and carrier assigned (e.g., FedEx, UPS, DHL).
3. If the carrier tracking shows estimated delivery within the normal window, the agent must provide the latest tracking checkpoint and reassure the customer.

## Section 2: Delayed Shipments and Investigation Protocol
1. A shipment is considered DELAYED if 7 business days have passed without delivery or if tracking hasn't updated for more than 72 hours.
2. For delayed shipments, the agent must initiate an inquiry with the carrier and notify the customer that an investigation is underway.
3. If tracking indicates "Delivered" but the customer denies receipt, the agent must verify the delivery address and advise checking with neighbors or building reception before opening an investigation.

## Section 3: Lost in Transit Declarations
1. If a shipment remains untracked or undelivered for 14 calendar days, it is declared LOST_IN_TRANSIT.
2. Customers with lost shipments are eligible for either a free replacement reshipment or a full refund upon approval.
