# ElectroVault Global - Damaged Items Policy
Document ID: damaged_goods_policy_v1
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## Section 1: Damaged on Arrival (DOA) Reporting
1. If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report the damage within 7 calendar days of delivery.
2. The customer must provide an order ID and a description of the physical damage.
3. For claims over $100 (10,000 minor units), clear photographic documentation or serial verification is required before initiating a replacement or refund.

## Section 2: Resolution Pathways for Damaged Goods
ElectroVault offers two resolution pathways:
1. Pathway A - Expedited Replacement: A replacement order is created immediately with complimentary expedited shipping upon receipt of initial evidence.
2. Pathway B - Direct Refund: If the item is out of stock or the customer prefers not to receive a replacement, a refund proposal is created for human review and approval. Return of the damaged item may be waived if the salvage value is negligible or return shipping is disproportionately expensive.

## Section 3: High-Value Damaged Goods Escalation
Any damage claim involving an order exceeding $500 (50,000 minor units) cannot be handled automatically and must be escalated to Tier 2 Customer Support with priority HIGH.
